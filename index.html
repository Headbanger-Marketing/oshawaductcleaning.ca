FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Pricing, certification, timing, and the air itself, covered below.

How often do Oshawa homes need duct cleaning?

Plan on two to three years for the older bungalow and split stock, and three to four for newer north-end builds. Rentals near the schools are best done between tenancies, which keeps the interval honest.

Are your technicians certified and insured?

Certified and insured on every job in Oshawa and Durham. Ontario does not issue a trade licence for duct cleaning, so certification, training, and insurance are the credentials that mean anything here, and we carry all three.

How do I know the cleaning actually happened?

Photos from inside your own ducts, before and after, shown on site before we pack up. In a city full of older steel, the before photo is usually evidence enough on its own.

Our dryer heats but nothing dries. What is going on?

That combination points to a restricted vent, not a failing machine. We brush the run to the exterior hood, measure airflow there, and tell you plainly if the appliance itself is the issue.

What does the full cleaning involve and how long does it take?

Registers out and washed, negative-pressure vacuum at the furnace, each run brushed under suction, blower compartment cleaned, photos throughout. A typical Oshawa bungalow finishes inside three hours.

Can you clear the smell from years of tenants or smoke?

Yes. The cleaning removes the residue carrying the odour, then a sanitizer treatment applied to the clean runs addresses what is left. Ordered that way, the result holds instead of fading.

Why can we never stay ahead of the dust?

Return-side duct leaks, usually, drawing unfiltered basement air into the system across every run. We test for leakage and seal it, which is what finally gets a Oshawa household off the dust cloth treadmill.

Where beyond Oshawa do you work?

Across Durham, Whitby, Brooklin, Courtice, Bowmanville, Pickering, and Port Perry, with Oshawa crews out daily.

Do you serve areas outside of Oshawa, Ontario?

Yes. We serve Oshawa and nearby communities including Whitby, Courtice, Bowmanville, Brooklin, and the surrounding area.
